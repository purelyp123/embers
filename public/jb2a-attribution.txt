Media files are downloaded unchanged from the official free library.
Some files are copied under legacy Embers filenames for compatibility:
- Arms of Hadar and two Entangle transparency variants use normal-opacity
  originals because their custom 75% opacity files are unavailable.
- The Detect Magic cone thumbnail uses the free 5-foot cone thumbnail.
- The green Overcharged Sphere effect uses the free dark-purple animation
  and thumbnail because the custom green recolor is unavailable.

The asset manifest in Embers records each compatibility source and checksum.
